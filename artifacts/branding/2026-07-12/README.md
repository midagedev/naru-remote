# Naru Remote icon exploration — 2026-07-12

## Selected concept: Between Worlds

Two separate graphite planes become adjacent along one Signal-Blue seam. The
small off-white tile is a completed thought crossing from local composition to
remote action. It replaces the literal node-topology diagram with an abstract
threshold that can stand for text, voice, images, and files.

`between-worlds-imagegen-source.png` is the retained built-in Codex imagegen
source. `scripts/generate-app-icon.swift` normalizes it into the shipping
1024×1024 opaque sRGB asset. The retained visual proof is limited to
`first-screen-between-worlds-light.png`,
`first-screen-between-worlds-dark.png`, and
`springboard-new-icon-search.png`. Rejected generations and the superseded
shipping icon are intentionally omitted from the repository.

## Final generation prompt

> Create a second entirely new metaphysical icon concept for Naru Remote
> called “Between Worlds.” The product moves a finished thought from the local
> device into a distant private computer. Depict the instant when two otherwise
> separate spaces briefly overlap; avoid all literal computing imagery. Use an
> opaque full-bleed near-black graphite #111318 background. Show two large,
> simple rounded planes suspended in impossible perspective, one shifted
> slightly up-left and the other down-right. Their overlap opens into one clean
> Signal Blue #2D7DFF diagonal seam. A single small off-white rounded-square
> thought is split precisely by that seam, half in one plane and half in the
> other. Use original abstract metaphysical minimalism, matte vector-like
> surfaces, restrained dimensional shading, generous negative space, and a
> silhouette that remains clear at 60px. Do not include text, letters, arrows,
> devices, nodes, network lines, clouds, dot grids, infinity loops, glossy
> plastic, excessive bloom, an outer rounded-square mask, or a watermark.

## Launch treatment

The launch screen intentionally remains the adaptive canvas color only:
`#F7F8F5` in light appearance and `#111318` in dark appearance. This matches the
first real frame and avoids a branded splash that flashes away for returning
users, in line with Apple's launch-screen guidance.
